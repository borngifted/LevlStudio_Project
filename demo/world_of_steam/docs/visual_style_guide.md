# Visual Style Guide

## Design Pillars

- **Technology as spectacle**: 11G begins as beautiful public-facing wonder, then becomes extractive and unstable.
- **Learning as survival**: Every STEAM discipline should appear as a physical force that can repair, distort, or reshape the world.
- **Broadcast control versus hacked truth**: Corporate/palace media is clean and symmetrical; Endpoint's transmissions are interrupted, layered, and urgent.
- **The world reacts**: Terrain, equations, sound, and machines should visibly respond to character choices.

## Palette

| Use | Color Direction |
| --- | --- |
| 11G promise | gold, bright white, polished blue, clean glass |
| 11G failure | dim amber, dead city gray, blackout blue, warning red |
| Endpoint | electric blue, white scanlines, transparent cyan |
| World of STEAM | brass, copper, graphite, black iron, glowing teal |
| Fractal Trial | violet, cyan, bright equation-white, warning orange |
| STEAM Palace | cold brass, black marble, desaturated green, controlled white |

Avoid making the world only brown/bronze. Brass should be balanced with blue holograms, black industrial forms, colorful cultural motifs, and high-contrast trial lighting.

## Environments

### 11G City Network

Large city skylines should be dark enough for the 11G light web to read clearly. Use elevated camera moves, holographic UI reflections, and light beams converging into a sky vortex.

### Science Summit

The summit should feel sleek, international, and credible. The central holographic Earth is the production anchor. When the failure begins, the world map should fracture into glowing shards before the room blacks out.

### Summoning Portal

The portal sequence should feel like each teen's real-world discipline is being pulled apart: water pressure breaks for Aiesha, code dissolves for Diana, machinery rewrites around Qawnzy, music fragments around Carlos, equations scatter around Pyra, blueprints fold around Alex, and drone control fails around Aiko.

### World of STEAM Mechanical Landscape

Use living machinery at landscape scale: gears under translucent ground, towers that fold from equation lines, bridges that form only when solved, and surfaces that pulse with audience-like attention.

### First Trial Arena

The arena should support three intercut challenge zones:

- Fractal stabilization for Aiesha, Diana, and Pyra.
- Broken sound reconstruction for Carlos.
- Adaptive machine control for Qawnzy and Aiko.

### STEAM Palace

The palace should communicate control, hierarchy, and monitored creativity. Keep it grand but cold: precise columns, repeating formulas, compliant announcement screens, and Deduca framed above or behind the teens.

## Camera Language

- Commercial and propaganda: centered, smooth, clean, symmetrical, slightly too perfect.
- Endpoint transmissions: jittered framing, scanline overlays, broken aspect masks, intermittent black frames.
- Selection montage: kinetic push-ins and upward pull shots as beams claim each teen.
- First Trial: low-angle obstacle reveals, lateral problem-solving tracking shots, and overhead geometry shots for fractal logic.
- Deduca: controlled slow push, long lens compression, and static authority framing.

## Pipeline Notes

- Use Unreal/Blender for blockout geometry and camera staging before AI stylization.
- Use ComfyUI reference-to-video passes after the scene silhouette and lighting are locked.
- Keep fixed seeds and consistent reference sheets for the seven protagonists to avoid identity drift.
- Generate concept plates before model generation so character costume language stays tied to discipline and source setting.
- First renders can be 1080p previews; reserve higher-quality output for selected proof scenes.
