# World of STEAM Storyboard and Concept Prompts

Use these prompts for concept art, storyboard plates, scene references, and ComfyUI reference images. They are production prompts, not final dialogue.

## Style Prefix

Use this prefix for most cinematic plates:

`cinematic 3D animated science-fantasy, premium stylized feature animation quality, expressive young protagonists, steampunk machinery, holographic educational technology, dramatic volumetric lighting, readable character silhouettes, high-detail environment design, no text overlays`
